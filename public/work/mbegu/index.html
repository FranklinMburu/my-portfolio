Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Saving, round-ups, goals, chamas and withdrawals to M-Pesa are all free.

Can I take my money out at any time?

Yes. Withdraw to M-Pesa whenever you like. Most withdrawals arrive in seconds.

How is my account protected?

Your PIN or fingerprint is needed for every withdrawal, and new phones must be approved from your old one or by our support team.

Do I need a bank account?

No. All you need is an M-Pesa line and your national ID.
