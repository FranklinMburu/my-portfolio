How it works

  1. Tell us how you live

    Your commute, your budget, school runs, whether you need a garden for the dog. Twenty minutes on the phone.

  2. We visit first

    An agent walks every shortlisted home, checks the water, the power backup and the road in the rain.

  3. You view three, not thirty

    One morning, three homes that fit. We handle the offer, the lawyer and the keys.
