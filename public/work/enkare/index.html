The tents

Canvas walls, a timber deck and a view that runs to the escarpment. Each tent faces the river and none faces another.

  • King-size bed facing the plains
  • Outdoor shower and a copper bath
  • Private deck with day beds
  • Solar power, filtered river water

A day at Enkare

  1. 05:30

    Balloon over the plains

    Lift off in the dark and watch the light arrive from a thousand feet up. Champagne breakfast where you land.

  2. 09:00

    Slow morning on the deck

    Coffee from the highlands, binoculars on the river. Hippos keep the time.

  3. 15:30

    Afternoon game drive

    Open vehicles, one guide for every four guests, no rush to be anywhere else.

  4. 18:15

    Sundowners on the ridge

    The herds move toward the water. You stay until the colour goes.

  5. 20:00

    Dinner by the fire

    Three courses under the stars, cooked over acacia wood. Askari walk you home.

Your neighbours are lions.

The Talek pride hunts within sight of camp. The great migration crosses from July to October.
