Admissions

We admit throughout the year when places allow. Most families hear back within two weeks.

  1. 1

    Visit us

    Walk the campus on a school day and meet the head of your child's section.

  2. 2

    Apply online

    A short form and your child's last two reports. No application fee.

  3. 3

    A morning with us

    Your child joins a class for a morning. It's relaxed, and it isn't a test.

  4. 4

    Your offer

    We confirm a place and the start date, and send everything you need for day one.
