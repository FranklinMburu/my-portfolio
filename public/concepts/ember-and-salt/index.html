Ember & Salt

An open-fire kitchen in Westlands, Nairobi

Everything here meets the fire.

Beef, goat, tilapia, maize and plantain, cooked over acacia charcoal by people who know when to leave it alone.

One open kitchen. One long fire. A short menu that changes with the market at Wakulima.

The fire

Sit at the fire

Eight seats face the hearth. You watch your dinner cook, you talk to the people cooking it, and you leave smelling faintly of woodsmoke.

The dining room behind is quieter, for long tables and slow evenings.
