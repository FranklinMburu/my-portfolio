NGUVU

Strong is a practice, not a look.

Nguvu means strength in Swahili. We teach it in groups of eight, with coaches who learn your name and your limits before they add weight to the bar.

Barbells, bags, kettlebells and a track out back. Show up three times a week and we'll handle the rest.

Five sessions a week

Conditioning

Kettlebells, sleds and rowers. Forty minutes, done properly.

Eight sessions a week
